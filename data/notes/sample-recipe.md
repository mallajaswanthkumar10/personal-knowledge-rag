# Weeknight Lentil Soup

Ingredients: 1 cup red lentils, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 garlic cloves,
1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 4 cups vegetable stock, salt to taste.

Steps:
1. Saute diced onion and carrots until soft, about 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic, cumin, and turmeric, cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add lentils and stock, simmer 20 minutes until lentils are soft.
4. Blend half the soup for a creamier texture, then stir back in.

Total time: about 30 minutes. Freezes well for up to 3 months.
